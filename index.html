Memecoin rules (paper, wide-open): skip a coin only if the creator wallet is the #1 holder (pools, burn & lock accounts ignored) or pool liquidity < $10K. Top-10 concentration is a warning only. Stops: -6% hard, 6% trail after +12%.
Anti-impulse rules (paper): no rebuy for 2h after an exit (6h after a loss worse than -20%), never rebuy in the same cycle it was sold, skip if up >100% in 1h or pair <30 min old, must pass the score bar 2 cycles in a row, cancel if the fill slips >5% above the signal price, max 3 buys per cycle, keep 20% cash in reserve. Exits are never blocked.
No-panic-sell rules (paper): stop widens with volatility (2.5× recent swing, between -6% and -20%); a stop hit is re-checked after 75s and only sold if still below (else "wick, held"); 10-min grace after buying; trail after +12% is 6–15% based on volatility. Emergency exits always fire: -25% from entry, pool liquidity -50%, or pair gone.
